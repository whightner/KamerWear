# Code of Conduct

KamerWear aims to keep collaboration respectful, useful and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and participants should:

- communicate respectfully and professionally;
- critique ideas and code rather than people;
- welcome questions and different experience levels;
- respect privacy and consent;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats and personal attacks;
- avoid publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, hate speech, sexual harassment, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, repeated disruptive behavior, or abuse of project channels is not acceptable.

## Scope

This applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces where someone is representing KamerWear.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, hide or remove content and restrict participation when behavior is harmful or persistently disruptive.

Security concerns should be reported through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not debated publicly when disclosure could put users at risk.
